\caption{Three of the 1{,}000 test spectra, chosen by how strongly their 23 terms cancel:
weak ($\sigma/\sum_i|C_ia_i| \approx 1$), typical and strong; in each case the spectrum with the
median mean $-2\ln\lambda$ within its fifth of the cancellation distribution.
Top: POWHEG (points) and the smooth model with its fit and total ($\pm1\sigma$) bands.
Middle: the likelihood-ratio statistic of each bin,
$-2\ln\lambda = -2\ln[\mathrm{Poisson}(n;\mu)/\mathrm{Poisson}(n;n)]$, with $n$ the POWHEG event count and
$\mu$ the model prediction; for a correct model it follows $\chi^2_1$, with mean 1 (solid line)
and 95\% point 3.84 (dashed).
Bottom: the pull $z = (n-\mu)/\mathrm{sd}$ with sd the total uncertainty (filled) and without the
model-error term (open); the bands mark $|z|<1$ and $|z|<2$.
The likelihood-ratio statistic measures the quality of the prediction; the pull, the quality of its uncertainty.}
